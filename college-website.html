Who We Are

About the College

D.R. Mane Mahavidyalaya, Kagal, has grown from a modest local college into a trusted institution serving students from Kagal town and the surrounding rural belt. We remain affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur, and follow its prescribed curriculum across all faculties.

Our focus stays on accessible, quality higher education — supporting first-generation learners with a faculty that knows most students by name, not roll number.

  • NAAC accredited institution
  • Well-stocked central library with reading hall
  • Active NSS and sports department
  • Scholarship guidance cell for eligible students
  • Regular guest lectures and career counselling sessions
